    Corner names

    Only used when students walk to corners. Match them to your room, like Door or Windows. Leave blank to use numbers only.

    Sound check

    Plays the countdown, the bell and a corner call so you can set your speaker volume. Works even when sound is off.

    Quiz questions

    One question per line. Put the right answer first, then up to three wrong ones, each after a | bar. The answers get shuffled into the corners. Two answers works for true or false. Each class has its own list.

    Start a new game?

    Everyone comes back in and the calls clear. This can’t be undone.

    Student check-in

    Students check in on their own devices, then you seat only the students who are here. During the game, each student’s screen shows whether they’re in or out. When students stay seated, they pick their number on their screen, and anyone who picked the called number is marked out for you.
